# Akto: Security for AI Agents, MCP Servers and LLMs

Discover, test and protect every AI agent in your organization: the ones your employees use and the ones your teams build.

[Website](https://www.akto.io) • [Docs](https://ai-security-docs.akto.io) • [Blog](https://www.akto.io/blog) • [Contact](https://www.akto.io/contact-us)

---

## Why Akto

AI agents don't behave like traditional apps. They pick tools at runtime, call MCP servers, handle sensitive data and act on a user's behalf, often without security teams knowing they exist. Traditional AppSec, cloud security and DLP tools weren't built to see or control that.

Akto secures AI where it runs, with two products:

| | **Akto Atlas** | **Akto Argus** |
|---|---|---|
| **Secures** | AI used by employees on their devices | AI your company builds and runs |
| **Covers** | Coding agents, IDEs, AI chat apps, browser extensions, local models | Homegrown agents, MCP servers and GenAI apps in your cloud or on-prem |
| **Core capabilities** | Shadow AI discovery, posture, NHI governance, endpoint guardrails | Discovery, automated red teaming, runtime guardrails |
| **Docs** | [Atlas docs →](https://ai-security-docs.akto.io/akto-atlas-agentic-ai-security-for-employee-endpoints/overview) | [Argus docs →](https://ai-security-docs.akto.io/akto-argus-agentic-ai-security-for-homegrown-ai/overview) |

---

## Akto Atlas: Agentic AI security for employee endpoints

Atlas secures the AI agents, MCP servers and GenAI tools your employees use, without getting in the way of their work.

- **Agentic AI discovery:** a continuously updated inventory of agents, MCP servers, LLMs, skills and plugins, mapped to each employee and device.
- **AI security posture:** one risk dashboard for agentic assets, exploits, sensitive-data events and guardrail coverage, mapped to frameworks such as MITRE ATLAS, CIS Controls, FedRAMP and CMMC.
- **NHI governance:** tracks the API keys, tokens and secrets that agentic tools create, with expiry tracking, rotation policies and violation alerts.
- **Atlas Guardrails:** inspects prompts, responses and tool calls on the device to block sensitive-data exposure, prompt injection, risky MCP tool calls, shadow AI and sign-ins with personal accounts.

**Works with:**

- **Agentic CLIs and IDEs:** Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Copilot, Kiro, Cursor, VS Code and more
- **AI apps:** Claude, ChatGPT and Microsoft 365 Copilot
- **Browser extensions:** Chrome, Firefox and Safari
- **Local models:** Ollama, vLLM and others

**Deployment:** your existing EDR or MDM (CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Microsoft Defender, Intune, Jamf and others), or a hosted or on-prem proxy.

📖 [Atlas documentation](https://ai-security-docs.akto.io/akto-atlas-agentic-ai-security-for-employee-endpoints/overview)

---

## Akto Argus: Agentic AI security for homegrown AI

Argus secures the AI agents, MCP servers and GenAI apps your teams build, from CI/CD through production.

- **Discovery:** finds AI agents, MCP servers and GenAI apps across your cloud environments and keeps the inventory current across dev, staging and prod.
- **Automated red teaming:** continuously scans agents and MCP servers in CI/CD with 4,000+ AI-specific probes. Tests cover prompt injection, tool misuse, unsafe actions, policy bypass and new attack patterns.
- **Runtime guardrails:** define and enforce what agents may do in production, including which tools, actions, topics and data they can reach.
- **Integrations:** 50+ connectors, eBPF-based visibility, cloud-native integrations, and agent platforms such as AWS Bedrock, Databricks and n8n. You can also route traffic through Akto's MCP Gateway or AI Gateway for central control.

📖 [Argus documentation](https://ai-security-docs.akto.io/akto-argus-agentic-ai-security-for-homegrown-ai/overview)
